## Our Engineering Principles

- **Vendor Neutrality** — technology choices guided by customer requirements, not cloud lock-in.
- **Interoperability** — connecting existing platforms, databases, and external systems.
- **Security by Design** — access controls, confidentiality, and traceability.
- **Verifiable Information** — data provenance, evidence, and explicit uncertainty.
- **Modular Architecture** — systems designed for integration, evolution, and independent deployment.
